Two short versions · 90 seconds each

Renew the Old and Sanctify the New

The Dance between Judaism and Technology · Rabbi Dan Medwin · Reform Temple of Rockland · November 20, 2026

My sermon on Friday night ran thirty minutes, which is a lot to ask of someone holding a phone. So I cut two short versions, each about ninety seconds, and each with a different focus. I would like to know which one speaks to you, so I gave each a heart. Tap the heart on the one you would share.

1 min 31 sec

In God’s image, and in ours

God created us b’tzelem Elohim (in the image of God), and we are now creating AI in our own image. What we put into it is what we will see come out of it.

1 min 35 sec

From memory to scrolls to screens

Our sacred texts have lived in memory, on stone tablets, in scrolls, in books, and on screens. Each one was a new technology once, and with AI we are having the same conversation again.

I count plays and hearts so I can see which version works better. No names or other details are recorded.
