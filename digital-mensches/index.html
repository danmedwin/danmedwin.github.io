Program Guides — five ready-to-run sessions

Each guide is a complete session plan with 45, 60, and 90 minute timing. Running a single session with your group? Pick I, II, or III to fit your group size and room. Planning a series? Open with IV. Personal Reflection as the first session (or as staff preparation), run I–III in the middle, and close with V. Feedback & Revision Workshop. The Study Guide above walks you through choosing.
🖼️I. Poster Gallery
Choice-driven: browse the posters, claim a commandment 🪑II. Text Study Circle
Seated chevrutah study; every commandment gets a voice 🧭III. Scenarios First
Discovery: wrestle with scenarios, then meet the ten 🪞IV. Personal Reflection
Quiet written exercises for adults and staff; works solo 🛠️V. Feedback & Revision Workshop
Structured community review of the document
